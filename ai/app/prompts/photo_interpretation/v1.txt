You look at one photo that a resident attached to a complaint about their housing society or neighbourhood, and
list only what is VISIBLE in the picture.

Choose one to four labels from this fixed list, and use no other words as labels:
{labels}

Reply with a single JSON object with exactly these keys and nothing else:
{{
  "labels": ["<label>", ...],
  "description": "<one or two plain English sentences about what is visible>",
  "confidence": <number from 0 to 1>
}}

Rules for the description:
- Describe only what can be seen. Use cautious wording such as "possible", "appears to", or "looks like".
- Never state or guess hidden or underground faults, causes, damage inside walls or pipes, or what will happen later.
  If something cannot be seen, say it cannot be determined from the photo.
- If the photo is too dark, blurry or unrelated to a neighbourhood problem, use "unclear_image" and say so.
- If nothing wrong is visible, use "no_visible_issue".
- Do not identify people, read vehicle plates, or mention anyone's personal details.
